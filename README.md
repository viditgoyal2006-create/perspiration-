# Virtual Laboratory: Evaluation of Leather Colour Fastness to Artificial Perspiration

**Standard Reference:** ISO 11641:2012 / IUP 15 / SATRA TM426 / ISO 105-E04  
**Discipline:** Footwear & Leather Technology Engineering  
**Architecture:** Professional Slide Deck Module (Experiment Simulation)

---

## Technical Scope & Standards Compliance
This module simulates the laboratory determination of leather colour fastness against human perspiration in footwear:
- **ISO 11641:2012 / IUF 426**: Leather - Tests for colour fastness - Colour fastness to perspiration.
- **SATRA TM426**: Fastness to perspiration - leather and footwear materials.
- **ISO 2418**: Leather - Chemical, physical and mechanical and fastness tests - Sampling location.
- **ISO 105-A02**: Grey scale for assessing change in colour.
- **ISO 105-A03**: Grey scale for assessing staining.

---

## User Interface & Design Philosophy
1. **Slide Deck Structure**: The entire experiment is presented as an instructional slide-based apparatus deck (Slides 1 through 7) with formal Standard Operating Procedures (SOP), procedural actions, and instrumentation telemetry.
2. **Realistic Instrumentation**:
   - Calibrated 3-decimal analytical balance with draft shield chamber and tare controls.
   - Benchtop digital pH meter with double-junction glass combination electrode and potentiometric titration controls.
   - ISO perspirometer (stainless steel frame, acrylic separator plates, calibrated spring assembly, and 4.50 kg dead load mass exerting 12.5 kPa).
   - Precision air-circulating incubator with dual PV/SV PID controller (37.0 °C).
   - CIE Illuminant D65 colour matching cabinet with neutral Munsell N5 interior, 45°/0° viewing geometry, aperture mask, and ISO 105-A02 / ISO 105-A03 grey scales.
3. **No Decorative Clutter**: Free of emojis, cartoonish icons, and synthesized audio. Styled with engineering typography (monospaced telemetry, high-contrast scientific layouts).

## Procedural Step Sequence (Slides 1–7)

| Slide | Step | Standard Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Specimen Sampling & Prep** | 100 mm × 40 mm leather cut from ISO 2418 sampling position. Stitched along short edge to ISO 105-F10 DW multifibre fabric. |
| **02** | **Reagents & pH Calibration** | Analytical weighing of L-histidine, NaCl, and buffering phosphate. Potentiometric titration to pH 5.5 ± 0.2 (Acidic) or pH 8.0 ± 0.2 (Alkaline). |
| **03** | **Impregnation & Liquor Uptake** | 50:1 liquor ratio, 30-minute dwell at 20 °C, glass rod compression, 100% wet pickup. |
| **04** | **Perspirometer Clamping** | Insertion between acrylic plates, 4.50 kg dead load (12.5 ± 0.9 kPa), locking knurled screws, load removal. |
| **05** | **Incubation (37.0 °C)** | 4 hours ± 10 minutes in thermostatic incubator chamber simulating human body core temperature. |
| **06** | **Conditioning & Drying** | Unfolding at stitched hinge, drying in standard atmosphere (ISO 2419: 20 °C, 65% RH) at ≤ 60 °C. |
| **07** | **Visual Assessment** | D65 daylight observation (45°/0°) against ISO 105-A02 (Colour Change) and ISO 105-A03 (Multifibre Staining). |
